Calculation Method

Step 1 – Wet Plaster Volume
Wet plaster volume is calculated as:

Vwet = Area × Thickness

Step 2 – Increase Wet Volume by 30%
The wet plaster volume is increased by 30% to obtain the dry material volume:

Vdry = Vwet × 1.30

Step 3 – Apply Wastage
Vfinal = Vdry × (1 + Wastage/100)

Step 4 – Divide According to Mix Proportion
For example, for a 1 : 4 plaster mix:

Cement = 1 / (1 + 4) × Final Volume
Sand = 4 / (1 + 4) × Final Volume

Step 5 – Convert Volume into Mass
Material Quantity = Material Volume × Material Density

Step 6 – Cement Bags
Number of 50 kg bags = Cement Quantity ÷ 50

About the Calculator

This calculator estimates the quantity of cement and fine aggregate/sand required for plastering work based on the plastering area, plaster thickness, mix proportion, material density and wastage.

The wet plaster volume is first calculated from the plastering area and plaster thickness. The wet volume is then increased by 30% to obtain the dry material volume.

The specified wastage percentage is subsequently applied to the dry material volume. The resulting volume is used for material estimation.

Example plaster mix: 1 : 4
